# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis & Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing patterns, evaluate product category performance, and compare store performance.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and transaction trends.
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Understand hourly and daily sales patterns.
- Compare revenue across different store locations.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Present key performance indicators through an interactive dashboard.

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains **149,116 transaction records** covering the period from January 1, 2023, to June 30, 2023.

**Key dataset attributes:**
- Transaction ID
- Transaction Date and Time
- Store Location
- Product Category
- Product Type
- Product Details
- Transaction Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Bill
- Month and Day Information

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The interactive dashboard provides insights into:

1. **Hourly Sales Analysis:** Examines order patterns across different hours of the day.
2. **Product Category Analysis:** Evaluates the contribution of different categories to total revenue.
3. **Order Size Distribution:** Analyzes the distribution of product sizes.
4. **Store Performance:** Compares sales and transaction performance across store locations.
5. **Top 5 Products:** Identifies the highest-revenue products.
6. **Weekday Analysis:** Examines transaction activity across different days of the week.

**Interactive Filters:**
- Month Name
- Day Name

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Coffee generated approximately 38.63% of total revenue, making it the largest revenue-contributing category.
- Tea contributed approximately 28.11% of total revenue.
- Coffee and tea together accounted for approximately 66.74% of total sales.
- Hell's Kitchen recorded the highest revenue among the three store locations, generating $236,511.17.
- 10 AM recorded the highest hourly sales revenue.
- Friday recorded the highest number of transactions, with 21,701 transactions.
- Monday recorded the highest daily revenue, generating $101,677.28.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

- Maintain strong inventory availability for coffee and tea products.
- Evaluate opportunities to promote complementary bakery products.
- Review staffing and inventory requirements during peak sales hours.
- Study the performance differences between store locations to identify improvement opportunities.
- Use weekday sales trends to plan inventory and promotional activities.
